Privacy policy

Last updated 28 September 2026. HUUMAN Studio follows Thailand's Personal Data Protection Act B.E. 2562 (PDPA).

What we collect

  • The details you send us through the consultation form or by email: your name, property, email address and website.
  • Messages you exchange with us about a project.

Why we use it

Only to answer your request, prepare your audit and proposal, and deliver work you commission. We do not sell or share your data for marketing.

How long we keep it

For as long as we have a working relationship, and afterwards only as long as Thai law requires for accounting.

Guest data for our clients

When we build a concierge or booking system for a property, we act as a data processor and sign a data processing agreement with the property owner.

Your rights

You may ask to see, correct or delete your personal data, or withdraw consent, at any time by writing to marketingwsean@gmail.com.
