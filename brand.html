01 The idea

HUUMAN is human, with one extra letter. The first U is us. The second, drawn in bronze, is you: the owner, the guest, the person on the other side of the screen.

The letters H, U and U share their stems, so the name reads as one continuous gesture. Nothing in the mark stands alone. That is how we work with the properties we serve.

Every line has the same weight and ends with a clean, flat cut, like joinery in teak or a stone threshold. The A drops its crossbar and becomes an open doorway. It is quiet at a glance and rewarding up close.

02 Construction

Four stems, one rhythm

H, U and U sit on four equally spaced stems, 72 units apart. Two stems are shared, which is what makes the mark ownable.

One line weight

A single stroke of 7 units on a cap height of 100. The mark stays crisp from a favicon to a building facade.

An open A

The A has no crossbar. It reads as an arch, an entrance, a place you are welcome to walk into.
