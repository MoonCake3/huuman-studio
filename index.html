Questions

What owners ask us first.

How much does a commission cost?

Every property is priced individually after the audit, based on your room rates, occupancy and how many enquiries you receive. You get three options with clear assumptions. There are no hidden monthly fees.

Will this replace Booking.com and Agoda?

No, and it should not. OTAs are good for discovery. Our goal is to move returning guests, referrals and social media traffic to direct bookings, where you keep the full rate.

Does the AI concierge replace my reservations team?

It handles the repetitive questions at any hour and hands over every serious enquiry with the details already collected. Your team spends time on guests who are ready to book.

What if the concierge gives a wrong answer?

It only answers from information you approve. When it is unsure, it says so and passes the guest to your team. You can read every conversation.

How long does it take to launch?

A Start commission usually launches within two to three weeks of approval. Larger sites take six to ten weeks, depending on photography and content.

Can you work with our existing booking engine?

Yes. We connect to most booking engines and channel managers used in Thailand. If yours has no connection, we design around it.

Do you provide photography?

We art direct every shoot and can arrange a photographer. When your existing images are strong, we work with them.

How do payments work?

Smaller commissions are paid in advance. Larger ones are paid half at approval and half at launch, by Thai bank transfer or PromptPay. Care plans are billed monthly in advance.
